## Specification review — 2026-10-10

For a constant source lasting `T`, each final Gaussian component of settling time `tau` must have accumulated mass `final_weight * clip((t - tau) / T, 0, 1)`. Its final horizontal center and variance depend on `tau`, not the display clock. This is the arrival-time integral of the existing simplified release model, not a time-dependent airborne concentration solver. Plume rise speed and visible parcel geometry must not silently alter the ash calculation.

The emitted mass is `Q * clip(t, 0, T)`. Deposited mass over the infinite plane is the sum of arrived component weights; airborne mass is emitted minus deposited mass. Finite-domain loss at that frame is deposited mass minus integrated in-domain deposition. Unemitted mass cannot be counted as airborne or outside the domain. Every frame must satisfy the same closure and eventual deposition must equal the existing final solver.

Emission stops at `T`, but the final ash frame occurs at `T + max(tau)` over every release layer and grain class. The midpoint trajectories displayed previously do not supply this maximum. A zero-ash scenario must not create ash plume mass, deposition or ash exposure. A Gaussian has no sharp physical hazard radius; contours are specified accumulated-deposit reporting levels, and municipal samples are centroid screening.

Lava routing must use unexaggerated source DEM elevations and metric neighbor distances. The 6× Taal close-up is presentation only. A D8 path is one assumed channel, with no terrain updating, branching, levee failure or thermal rheology. Pits, flats, water and a domain edge are routing limits; reaching one does not establish real lava extinction or downstream safety. An assumed channel-width buffer is not an official lava hazard zone.

## Independent primary-source review

- [Smithsonian GVP Taal profile and January 2020 reports](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=273070), reopened 2026-10-10, reports weak lava fountaining during 13 January and roughly 500 m fountains at north-flank vents. This supports illustrating a fountain with the historical context stated. It does not supply effusion rate, viscosity, channel width or a regional effusive-flow trajectory for this application. The reported precautionary evacuation radius is not an ash-model contour.
- [USGS HVO, 2019-02-28, “How do lava flows cool and how long does it take?”](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo/news/volcano-watch-how-do-lava-flows-cool-and-how-long-does-it-take), reopened 2026-10-10, explains the roles of thickness, initial temperature, heat loss, rainfall/wind and insulating crust. An isothermal Newtonian demonstration omits these controls. Its computed arrival times and artificial routing stops cannot certify observed runout or cooling duration.
- [Lagmay et al., 2021-08-03, “Hazardous base surges of Taal’s 2020 eruption”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-94866-2), reopened 2026-10-10, uses field/UAV/LiDAR observations to document hot, wet pyroclastic density currents. It also distinguishes gas-thrust, convective and umbrella plume regions. These provide research context for shape and omitted hazards, not validation of decorative cloud particles. A glowing lava channel cannot stand in for the reported base surges. The paper's linked archived PHIVOLCS January 13 bulletin returned HTTP 502 in this audit; the direct bulletin was not independently read.
- The search excerpt for [USGS Bulletin 1021-B](https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/1021b/report.pdf) describes a Jeffreys channel-flow approximation and its uncertainty. Direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 403 in this audit. That excerpt alone is **not recorded as full equation/source verification**; dimensional and synthetic tests of the implemented formula remain distinct from source applicability.

## Skill usefulness in this extension

The numerical and QA skills were useful in separating source-stop from eventual settling, emitted/aloft/domain-loss mass, flat ash datum from raw terrain, and dated hazard context from computed footprints. Synthetic valid counterexamples include zero ash without invented plume mass and unsupported lava remaining unavailable despite a requested fountain. Anti-slop guidance was useful in retaining one player, progressive disclosure, matching palette/selectors and honest display scales. These are scoped observed benefits. The initial slope-distance miss and lack of a final independent browser signoff show that the skills do not prove scientific accuracy by themselves; primary-source review and explicit arithmetic still matter.
