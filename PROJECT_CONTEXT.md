## Product objective

ABO is a Gaussian plume ashfall dispersion simulator for estimating municipal exposure from Philippine volcanic eruptions. The academic goal is to replicate a documented eruption footprint, then compare how eruption size and seasonal winds change ashfall at municipalities. Python computes the model; React presents controls, a deposit map, and interpretable exposure results.

The proposal names roof-collapse, crop-damage, water-supply contamination, and aviation concerns. Its general objective mentions aviation thresholds; detailed objectives and features additionally name water contamination. Track all four as separate intended outputs, while treating their criteria and scientific interpretation as unresolved.

## Confirmed proposal requirements

1. Evaluate an analytic advection-diffusion Gaussian plume expression on a 2D coordinate mesh. Do not iterate over grid cells in Python.
2. Use three to five grain-size classes with distinct settling velocities. Broadcast the class axis and sum contributions over that axis.
3. Model a steady eruption over a specified duration, including column height, mass eruption rate, total mass, uniform horizontal wind, and diffusion.
4. Use a regional domain of roughly 200 by 200 km, at 1 km resolution. Define cell-center and extent conventions before implementation.
5. Support Taal, Kanlaon, and Mayon with configurable vent coordinates.
6. Provide manually sourced amihan and habagat wind presets and manual speed/direction input.
7. Derive deposit thickness from ashfall load and evaluate municipality centroids using manually entered coordinates and populations.
8. Produce a thickness contour map with municipality markers, an exposure table, threshold flags, population counts, and an aviation screening indicator.
9. Provide a January 2020 Taal replication mode and compare its modeled footprint to sourced reported affected areas.
10. Sweep wind direction through 360 degrees to find each municipality's largest modeled exposure. Direction step and memory policy remain decisions to resolve.
11. Enter all environmental, eruption, municipality, and population data manually from published sources. The application must not fetch live scientific data or call external data APIs.

The no-API statement concerns scientific input collection. A local FastAPI interface between the Python calculation and React UI is an engineering choice that preserves manual data entry. Record this interpretation for course review if the instructor intended a prohibition on all HTTP interfaces.

## Explicit limitations

The proposal assumes a steady plume, uniform wind, and flat terrain. It excludes altitude-dependent wind shear, pulsed complex multi-lobe eruptions, ash aggregation, rain flushing, remobilization, lahars, pyroclastic density currents, base surges, ballistic projectiles, and volcanic gases.

Centroid results represent point samples; they are not municipality-wide deposit averages. Population totals based on centroid classification are indicative counts of residents in classified municipalities, not measured person-level exposure. Thresholds are generalized literature criteria, not building-level assessments.

Every result must state that it is an academic simulation and is not a PHIVOLCS hazard advisory. The proposal's account of the January 2020 Taal eruption is a source claim; the historical evidence and numerical replication still need independent data collection and validation.

## Units and numerical invariants

Use explicit SI units in Python contracts: height/distance in meters, rate in kg/s, duration in seconds, speed in m/s, load in kg/m2, density in kg/m3. Convert thickness to millimeters at a clearly named boundary. Preserve longitude/latitude ordering and projection metadata.

Require finite inputs and outputs, positive duration/wind speed/density where a formulation requires them, nonnegative mass, grain fractions normalized to one, explicit upwind and near-vent treatment, and bounded domain/sweep resources. Zero mass should yield a zero deposit. The total integrated deposit inside a finite domain must not exceed available erupted ash mass under the selected model; material transported outside the domain must be distinguished from a conservation defect.

Meteorological direction means the bearing the wind comes FROM, clockwise from north. Convert to a transport direction explicitly and test cardinal directions. An out-of-domain municipality is unknown/outside coverage, never silently zero or safe. Threshold population counts may overlap across hazards; do not sum them as unique residents.
